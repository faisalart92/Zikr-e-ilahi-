🔒 Privacy Policy

Zikr-e-Ilahi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Zikr-e-Ilahi is an Islamic utility app that provides Quran reading, prayer times, Qibla direction, duas, a tasbeeh counter and an Islamic calendar. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Information stored on your device

The app does not require an account, and it does not operate its own user account or advertising server. To remember your app settings and progress, it may store the following information locally on your device using browser/app storage: your saved location coordinates, last-read Quran position, bookmarked verses, tasbeeh count and settings, and daily prayer-tracker selections. These items remain on your device unless you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

2. Location permission

If you allow location access, the app uses your device's location to calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction. You can instead enter a location manually. The app saves the selected latitude and longitude on your device so it can show the relevant information next time. Location may be sent to the external services listed below when requesting prayer times, Qibla direction or place-name search results. You can deny location permission and use manual location entry where available.

3. External services

Some features need internet access and request information from third-party services:

  • AlQuran Cloud API — Quran text and related Quran content.
  • Aladhan API — prayer times and Qibla direction, using the selected location.
  • Photon / OpenStreetMap Nominatim — searching for a place name and converting it into location coordinates when you use location search.

When the app contacts these services, they may receive technical information such as your IP address and the query or coordinates needed to provide the requested result. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. The app developer does not control those services.

4. Notifications and alarms

If you enable prayer notifications or alarms, the app requests notification permission and may schedule prayer reminders on your device. On supported Android builds, the app may use the device's notification and exact-alarm features. You can turn reminders off in the app or change permissions in your device settings.

5. Sharing and selling

The app developer does not sell your personal information. The app does not intentionally send your bookmarks, tasbeeh count, Quran reading progress or prayer-tracker selections to the app developer. Information sent to third-party services is limited to what is needed for the feature you request, as described above.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general audiences. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system. Parents or guardians can manage device permissions and clear locally stored app data.

7. Your choices and data deletion

You can deny or revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings. You can clear locally stored app data through your Android app settings or browser settings; doing so may remove saved location, bookmarks, reading progress and counters. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or services change. The latest version will be available from the Privacy Policy page in the app.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app developer through the project's GitHub repository:

Zikr-e-Ilahi GitHub Repository

Note: This policy describes the app's current implementation and is not legal advice. Review it again if you add analytics, advertising, accounts, cloud backups or other data collection.
